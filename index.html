CAREER ZONE

Textile Career

Textile industry-তে বিভিন্ন career path সম্পর্কে জানুন।

01

Merchandising

Buyer communication, costing, order follow-up ও production coordination.

02

Quality Control

Fabric ও garment quality inspection এবং defect analysis.

03

Industrial Engineering

Production efficiency, SMV, line balancing ও productivity.

04

Production

Spinning, weaving, knitting, dyeing অথবা garments production.

05

CAD & Design

Fashion design, pattern making এবং CAD-based technical work.

06

Management

Experience ও skills-এর মাধ্যমে supervisory এবং management roles.
